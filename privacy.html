Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites.
  • If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address to send you two emails: one now to confirm your address, and one when Mossling launches, with your early-bird price. Nothing else.
  • Your address is stored by our email provider, Buttondown, in the United States. Our emails record whether they are opened and which links are clicked.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising. Unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data at any time: mossling@runivoxlabs.com.

1. Who we are

Mossling is made by Runivox LTD, trading as Runivox Labs, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 16980284. Our registered office is 20 Wenlock Road, London N1 7GU, United Kingdom.

Runivox LTD is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us about anything in it at mossling@runivoxlabs.com or by post at the address above.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Mossling website (currently mosslingapp.github.io, later mossling.app) and the Mossling waitlist. The Mossling app itself keeps your code and data on your Mac; it will come with its own privacy information when it is released.

3. What we collect and why

When you visit the website

The website itself does not set cookies, use local storage for tracking, run analytics or advertising scripts, or load fonts, images or scripts from other companies. Everything is served from our own site.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as it does for every GitHub Pages site. We do not receive or access these logs. GitHub handles them under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We rely on our legitimate interest in running a secure, reliable website (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

When you join the waitlist

When you submit the form, your browser sends the following directly to Buttondown, our email provider:

  • your email address;
  • the tag “waitlist”, so we know where you signed up.

Buttondown also records when you subscribed and confirmed, and may record technical details of the sign-up such as your IP address, to show that you asked to subscribe and to prevent spam and abuse.

We use your email address only to send you two emails:

  • a confirmation email, so we know the address is yours (you are only added once you confirm);
  • an email when Mossling launches, including your early-bird price.

We will not send you anything else. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Our legal basis is your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you give by submitting the form and confirming your address. This also covers the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, which require consent for marketing email. You can withdraw consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by writing to us.

Email opens and link clicks

Our emails contain a small invisible image and tracked links, provided by Buttondown. They tell us whether an email was opened and which links were clicked, along with the time and the type of device or email app used. We use this only in aggregate, to understand whether our emails are useful and working. We do not use it to build profiles or for advertising.

This happens as part of the emails you consented to receive. To stop it, you can block remote images in your email app, or unsubscribe. If you would like to stay on the list without this tracking, write to us and we will tell you what we can do.

When you reply to our emails

Our confirmation email invites you to tell us which tools you use. If you reply, Buttondown receives your reply and passes it on to us, so we get your email address, your name if your email shows it, and what you write. We use replies to decide which tools Mossling supports next and to answer you. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in building a product that fits the people waiting for it (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not use personal data for advertising or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not make decisions about you by automated means, including profiling, that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. International transfers

Buttondown and GitHub are based in the United States, so your data is transferred outside the UK and processed in the United States. Buttondown protects these transfers with the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses together with the UK Addendum to them, as set out in its data processing agreement. GitHub describes the safeguards it uses in its Privacy Statement. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Waitlist: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If you are still on the waitlist 12 months after Mossling launches, we delete your address then.
  • Unconfirmed sign-ups: addresses that are never confirmed are not added to the list and are deleted from Buttondown within 30 days.
  • Email open and click data: kept with your waitlist record and deleted with it.
  • Replies: kept for as long as we need them to act on your feedback, and no longer than 24 months.
  • Website security logs: kept by GitHub under its own policy.

When you ask us to delete your data, we do so within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict how we use your data;
  • object to our use of your data where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what we did before.

To use any of these rights, email mossling@runivoxlabs.com. It is free, and we will reply within one month. We may need to confirm it is you before acting on a request.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. If you live in the European Union, you can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

9. Security

The website is served only over HTTPS. Access to the waitlist is limited to the people at Runivox LTD who need it, and protected by strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication. Buttondown encrypts data in transit and at rest.

10. Children

Mossling is a tool for software developers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has joined the waitlist, contact us and we will delete their data.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will not apply it to you without your consent.
