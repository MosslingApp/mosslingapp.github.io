Questions from the field

Does Mossling touch my code?

It moves your work into a separate git worktree and backs it up first. It never deletes, resets or force-pushes anything.

What if something fails halfway through a park?

Mossling stops and leaves your desk as it was. The backup it took first lets you roll back in one step.

Which tools does it support?

At launch: Ghostty, VS Code, Cursor and Docker Compose with Postgres and Redis. iTerm2 and more come next, prioritized by what waitlist members use.

Will snapshots fill up my disk?

Mossling checks free space before every park. If there isn't enough room, it stops and tells you before touching anything.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything stays on your Mac. The optional AI note calls the model with your own API key.

Why isn’t it on the Mac App Store?

The App Store sandbox blocks access to terminals and Docker. Mossling ships as a signed, notarized download.
